Ube Banana

Where to eat, rated only by people who were really there.

Google reviews can be written by anyone, from anywhere. On Ube Banana you can only rate a restaurant while you’re at the restaurant. The app checks your location first.

Open the app iPhone Android

Why I built it

Every barkada and office has the same daily question: saan tayo kakain? Most of us stopped trusting Google ratings and just ask friends. Ube Banana puts the friends’ answer in one place, and makes sure every rating comes from a real visit.

Made in Manila by Nicolo Hallare. Free for diners and restaurants.

What’s in it

Real scoresA score shows only after 3 verified visits. Google’s rating sits beside it, never mixed in.
Every TikTok reviewFood creators’ TikTok and YouTube reviews of each place, on one page.
The MICHELIN listAll of MICHELIN Guide Philippines 2027, sorted by what’s near you.
Card promosBDO, BPI, Metrobank and other promos that apply where you’re eating.

How it stays honest

Location-checkedNo check-in unless you’re at the restaurant.
No paid scoresRestaurants can’t pay to change a score. Promos are always labelled.
Friends firstSee what the people you follow gave a place before anything else.

Try it on your next meal

Open it, find a place near you, and check in when you get there. It takes a minute.
